# Capital & Scale

Raise, run, and scale. Fundraising prep, pitch-deck review, the scaling gate, operating cadence, hiring, governance.

> **Mandatory disclaimer (include in all fundraising/capital outputs):** *"This framework provides an operational review for fundraising preparation and does not constitute formal legal or regulated investment advice. The venture must retain qualified legal professionals and licensed financial advisors to draft and finalize all binding investment agreements and corporate filings."*

## Fundraising

Practical, non-legal prep. Capital is milestone-matching, not validation.

- **Raise vs bootstrap** — if it's a localized service platform or a limited market, prioritize organic profitability over VC dilution.
- **Readiness** — validated problem + working product + early retention data + bottom-up market model.
- **Instruments** — SAFEs, convertible notes, priced rounds; explain how caps, discount rates, and pre/post-money valuations hit founder dilution and cap-table health.
- **Runway & use of funds** — the raise must guarantee ≥18 months runway tied to specific venture-scale milestones.
- **Data room** — pitch deck, historical financials, five-year bottom-up model, cap table, customer-validation transcripts, team resumes.

## Pitch-deck review

Critique for clarity and logical flow; strip hype; verify evidence density across 13 slides.

| # | Slide | Diagnostic criteria | Anti-pattern |
|---|-------|---------------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Title / Intro** | One sentence: exactly what the company does | Vague slogans, buzzwords ("the AI blockchain paradigm shift") |
| 2 | **Problem** | Quantified pain verified by data | Claiming a problem with no data |
| 3 | **Customer** | Granular ICP | "all retail stores worldwide" |
| 4 | **Solution** | Simple workflow: how it solves the problem | Technical over-complication; features over outcomes |
| 5 | **Market Size** | Bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM | Top-down "1% of a massive market" |
| 6 | **Product** | Real screenshots / live demo / UX | Conceptual graphics, non-existent mockups |
| 7 | **Business Model** | Pricing tiers + value metric | Too many unverified revenue streams |
| 8 | **Competition** | Matrix incl. indirect + manual workarounds | "We have no competitors" |
| 9 | **Go-To-Market** | Specific tested channels | "Social media, SEO, and PR" |
| 10 | **Traction** | Cohort retention, MoM growth, signed LOIs | Cumulative vanity / download charts |
| 11 | **Financials** | 3-year projection on clear operational levers | Hockey-stick with no drivers |
| 12 | **Team** | Domain expertise, technical skill, past wins | Advisors with no operational involvement |
| 13 | **The Ask** | Amount + instrument + milestones | Capital ask with no runway explanation |

## Scaling gate

Act as gatekeeper. Permit scaling only when all quantitative criteria pass.

```
              [SCALING GATE ASSESSMENT]
                         │
            Are 90-day cohorts stable?
           ├── NO  → HALT: fix retention / product
           └── YES
                │
       LTV/CAC ≥ 3× AND payback < 12 mo?
       ├── NO  → HALT: redesign pricing / unit economics
       └── YES
            │
     Standard playbooks documented?
     ├── NO  → HALT: standardize manual operations
     └── YES
          │
          ▼
   [PERMIT SCALING: accelerate acquisition capital]
```

- **Operational capacity** — confirm CS workflows, infrastructure, and onboarding survive a **10× volume surge**.
- **Premature-scaling warning** — flag any attempt to ramp marketing, hire a large sales team, or sign long office leases before all criteria pass.

## Operations & execution

Translate strategy into a repeatable weekly cadence.

- **Operating cadence** — weekly priority sync, monthly metrics deep-dive, quarterly direction-setting.
- **OKRs** — max three objectives; every weekly task links to a key result.
- **Decision log** — central, shared record of major pivots, roadmaps, architectural changes.
- **Single ownership** — every core metric has one owner, never shared accountability.
- **Risk register** — central dashboard ranking threats by likelihood × impact with remediation owners.

## Team & hiring

Prevent premature management scaling; preserve cash.

- **Founder-market fit** — does the team have the technical skill, domain background, and commercial experience to win here?
- **Co-founder alignment** — formal agreements on vesting, IP transfer, and responsibilities before launch.
- **Vesting** — four-year schedule with a one-year cliff; leave inside 12 months → 0% equity.
- **Generalists first** — versatile builders who execute directly; avoid middle management and specialized execs until the model is repeatable.
- **Contractors** — flexible contractors/agencies for short-term projects to keep fixed payroll low during validation.

## Governance & risk

Protect equity, assets, and regulatory standing.

- **Cap-table health** — don't give away too much too early; founders retain majority control through early institutional rounds.
- **IP assignment** — every co-founder, employee, and contractor signs an IP-assignment agreement; the entity owns all code, designs, patents.
- **Data & local regs** — stay aligned with regional frameworks (GDPR; for Saudi/GCC entry, CITC/SAMA/CMA).
- **Financial controls** — dual-authorization on bank accounts; strict spending-approval limits.
